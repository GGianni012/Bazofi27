With never-before-seen archive footage and interviews, it offers an intimate portrait interspersed with ballads and poems of love and melancholy. Taking a bus to La Plata, whose vibrant scene in the 1990s was and will always be unlike anything else. Ever. Understanding what happens when an artist who doesn’t yet know they are an artist begins to ignite from within. Neither the music nor the songs need explanation, of course, but there is something in this documentary that accurately conveys Moretti’s feverish side. An urgency that does not depend on virtuosity but on impulse. The story is emotional, endearing, and also hopeful—the antihero goes from being completely lost to discovering that doing what truly inspires him (writing songs) can save him.
