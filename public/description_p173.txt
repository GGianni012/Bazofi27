Santiago González Manuela’s routine is shaken when she is told that the apartment where she lives will be sold. Unable to avoid the change, she must face reality and, for the first time, take responsibility for her own life. This is a coming-of-age tale whose protagonist has to break free from the conformity of a borrowed present to face up to decisions. The short film relies on situational humor and the performance of its protagonist, whose deadpan tone makes us laugh and, at the same time, empathize with her. It is through her conflict (a universal one) that we understand what the main character is going through, which even makes us uncomfortable and happy at times. Incidentally, off-screen, we understand that the protagonist’s fear also stems from an uncertain economic future.
